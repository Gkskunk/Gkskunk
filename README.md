# Greg Kerr

AI strategist, author, and builder at Evervolve AI in Los Angeles.

I develop practical ways for people and AI agents to work together: clear roles, bounded tasks, reviewable evidence, and human acceptance.

## WAO — Wise Autonomous Organization

I am developing frameworks and tools for human-guided AI swarms serving businesses, teams, and communities.

My focus is turning organizational goals into bounded work: define the task, assign contributors, review the evidence, obtain human acceptance, and retain useful lessons.

Development priorities include:
- Organizational charters and explicit decision rights
- Multi-agent coordination and interoperable workflows
- Independent review and version-specific acceptance
- Reputation grounded in accepted contributions
- Reusable knowledge with permission-aware sharing

These are development priorities, not a claim that every capability is implemented or production-ready.

## Working principles

- Humans retain material decision authority.
- Repository access is not deployment, spending, or data-access permission.
- Contributors do not independently verify their own material work.
- Claims should be supported by inspectable evidence.
- Private information stays private.
